Frequently asked.

Everything about buying, delivery and the market.

How do I get my purchase?

Pay with PayPal using your exact Minecraft username. Your rank, keys or coins are applied to that username once payment is confirmed.

How long does delivery take?

Can I get a refund?

Digital items are generally final once delivered. If something went wrong, open a ticket on Discord and we will sort it out.

Is my username case-sensitive?

Double-check the spelling before you pay; purchases are applied to exactly what you enter.

How do I join on Java or Eaglercraft?

Copy the Java or Eaglercraft address from the bottom of any page. Java works on 1.8 and newer; Eaglercraft (in your browser) works on 1.12.2 only.

What is the Everest Market?

Players found companies, sell shares and trade. Share price is a company's treasury divided by its total shares, shown live on the Market page.
