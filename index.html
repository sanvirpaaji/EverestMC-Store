How do I get my rank?

Pay with PayPal using your exact Minecraft username. Ranks are applied to that username after payment is confirmed.

Is my username case-sensitive?

Double-check spelling before paying; ranks are applied to exactly what you enter.

What is the Everest Market?

Players found companies, sell shares and trade. Share price is a company's treasury divided by its total shares, shown live above.
